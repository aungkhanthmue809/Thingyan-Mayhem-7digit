How to Play

  1. Tap a person on screen to throw water at them.
  2. Read the mission bar at the top of the play area.
  3. အကြို — warm-up: splash 20 people in 20 seconds. Only friendly neighbours appear.
  4. အကျ — splash 6 men in red shirts. Splashing busy women or aunts costs a life (3 lives).
  5. အကြတ် — crowd challenge: splash 25 people in 45 seconds. Some walkers are faster.
  6. အတက် — final celebration: splash 30 people and reach a 5-person combo in 60 seconds. Chain splashes within 2.5 seconds to build a combo.
  7. Endless Mode — survive as long as you can. Splash everyone; anyone who walks past costs a life (5 lives). Every 20 splashes restores a life. Golden pedestrians are worth +5. Your best score is saved.
  8. Complete all four days to unlock Endless Mode.
